01 The idea

Storage should quiet a space, not compete with it.

The vertical profile makes thoughtful use of height, creating a natural home for everyday footwear without asking for a wide stretch of floor.

A warm wood finish softens the practical structure. Integrated LED lighting gives each level a soft glow in an entryway, bedroom, hallway, or closet.
